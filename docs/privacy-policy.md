# Privacy Policy for ZapFix

**Last Updated: June 12, 2026**

Welcome to ZapFix ("ZapFix", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the ZapFix mobile application and related services.

By using ZapFix, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you use ZapFix, we may collect:

* Name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Profile information
* Service address

### Location Information

We may collect your device location to:

* Match you with nearby service professionals
* Improve service availability
* Provide location-based services

Location data is collected only with your permission.

### Photos and Videos

Users may upload photos and videos of appliances or service issues for:

* AI-powered diagnosis
* Service request creation
* Communication with professionals

### Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect:

* Device type
* Operating system
* App version
* IP address
* Diagnostic and crash information
* Push notification tokens (to deliver job and service alerts)

### Professional Information

For service professionals, we may collect:

* Identity verification information
* Aadhaar or government-issued identification (where applicable)
* Bank account details (for payouts)
* Skill assessment information
* Business information

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

* Provide ZapFix services
* Generate AI-powered appliance diagnostics
* Connect users with service professionals
* Process bookings and payments
* Verify professional accounts
* Send service updates and push notifications
* Improve platform performance
* Prevent fraud and abuse
* Provide customer support
* Comply with legal obligations

## 3. AI Diagnosis

ZapFix may use artificial intelligence and machine learning systems to analyze uploaded images, videos, and descriptions of appliance issues.

AI-generated results are informational only and should not be considered professional technical advice.

Actual diagnoses and repairs may vary depending on the condition of the appliance.
